HackPit is plan, operate and go-deeper surfaces around one idea: the AI does the work, and you decide how much of it runs on its own. Here's everything it can do.

30+ surfaces155 tools2,747 KB techniques3 autonomy modes
01 // set the leash
default
mode 1

Manual

You approve every single command. The human is the wall — nothing runs without your click.

mode 2

Assisted

Passive recon and scans auto-run; every exploitation-class action is queued for you to approve or skip.

mode 3

Full auto

Everything fires on its own — bounded by your written Rules of Engagement, scope, a fire budget and a tamper-proof log.

Autonomy is off until you flip two switches. The repeater and anything dangerous never auto-fire, and a kill-switch halts it mid-run.
